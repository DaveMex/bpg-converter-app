About BPG Converter

Version 1.1.0

A cross-platform GUI tool for the Better Portable Graphics (BPG) format.

Converts images (PNG, JPG, WebP) to BPG and decodes BPG back to PNG/JPEG.

Powered by libbpg and Electron.

Developed with ❤️ by David Hernandez Mendez.

With the help of Google Antigravity and Gemini 3 pro.
